About Us

Learn more about Kinoti GK Hardware and our commitment to providing quality construction materials and tools.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Kinoti GK Hardware started as a small family business with a vision to provide quality construction materials to local builders. Over the years, we have grown to become one of the most trusted hardware suppliers in the region.

Our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction has been the cornerstone of our success. We believe in building lasting relationships with our clients by providing reliable products and exceptional service.

Today, we serve a wide range of customers from individual homeowners to large construction companies, providing them with the tools and materials they need to bring their projects to life.
